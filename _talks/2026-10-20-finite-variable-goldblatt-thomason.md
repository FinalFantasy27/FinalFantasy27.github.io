abstract: >-
  We characterize the frame definability of modal theories in which each formula contains at most n propositional variables. We introduce algebraic n-localizations and show that definability is characterized by closure under generated subframes, disjoint unions, and surjective bounded images, together with reflection of n-localizations. This provides a uniform algebraic framework for known results and new applications, including optimal variable bounds for bounded out-degree, common-successor confluence, density, width, and weak connectedness. We also address the global modality and relative definability over finite transitive frame classes.
