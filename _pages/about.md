I am a PhD student in Philosophy at the University of St Andrews. Before beginning my PhD, I completed an MA in Logic and Philosophy of Science at the Munich Center for Mathematical Philosophy, Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München.

My research interests include philosophical logic, modal logic, formal epistemology, metaphysics, and philosophy of science. My current work focuses on modal logic, noncontingency logic, representation theorems, and the philosophical applications of formal methods.

For publications, see the [publications page](/publications/). A complete curriculum vitae is available [as a PDF](/assets/pdf/CV.pdf).
