I published a popular-science article, [*The Dark Forest, Chains of Suspicion, and Game Theory* (黑暗森林、猜疑鏈與賽局理論)](https://www.math.sinica.edu.tw/mathmedia/journals/4997), in *Mathmedia*, a journal published by the Institute of Mathematics of the Academia Sinica, Taiwan. It uses the sci-fi novel *The Three-Body Problem* to introduce game theory and epistemic logic.
