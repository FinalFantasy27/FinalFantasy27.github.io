I published a popular-science article, [*The Dark Forest, Chains of Suspicion, and Game Theory* (黑暗森林、猜疑鏈與賽局理論)](https://www.math.sinica.edu.tw/mathmedia/journals/4997), in *Mathmedia*, a journal published by the Institute of Mathematics, Academia Sinica, Taiwan. Using *The Three-Body Problem* as a starting point, the article introduces game theory and epistemic logic.
